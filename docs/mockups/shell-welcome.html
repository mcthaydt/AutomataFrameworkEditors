Welcome to the editor

This is Crate Game's first level. You can change anything you see, and nothing goes in the game until you say so.

  1. 1Pick a thingTap the crate, the hero or the ground.
  2. 2Change itDrag it, or change its settings on the right.
  3. 3Put it in the gamePress the orange button at the top right when you are happy.

Takes a minute. You can see it again any time in Help.
