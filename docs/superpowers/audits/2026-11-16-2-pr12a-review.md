## Red: 3

### 1. With the sheet up and nothing picked, pressing a thing pulls the level out from under the finger

UNIT: `src/features/world/insets.ts`, `src/features/world/World.svelte` (the level's `bottom`).
VERDICT: red.
REQUIREMENT: `insetsOf`'s own rule, "picking one never moves the level under a finger that is dragging it"; touch first
(CLAUDE.md); the drunk-grandma test.
REASON: `sheet` is read from `lay.settings`, which is `sheet` only while something is picked, so the level's height
jumps the moment a press picks a thing.
EVIDENCE: phone, Crate Game: pick Crate 1, pull the sheet up, tap the empty sky (nothing picked, the sheet setting stays
"shown"): the level is 692 px high. Press on Crate 1: the level becomes 253.6 px and the crate's box moves from
y 466 to y 247 while the finger is still down at y 488. The drag that follows starts from a crate that is no longer
under the finger.
EXPECTED: a press and drag on a thing never moves the level under the finger; the level shrinks for the sheet only
when the person pulls it up (or once the drag ends), and the thing is still under the finger throughout.
FIX: decide `sheet` from the held setting (the `settings` argument, as `side` already is) or apply it after the
gesture ends; a journey check at `phone` and `ipadUpright` that a thing's box does not move between pointer down and
the first move with the sheet "shown" and nothing picked. Files: `insets.ts`, `World.svelte`, `insets.test.ts`, the
journey.

### 2. On a phone the toolbar's names are cut in seven languages, and in German and Russian Redo runs off the screen

UNIT: `src/features/shell/Toolbar.svelte`, the `max-width: 440px` rules.
VERDICT: red.
REQUIREMENT: CLAUDE.md "Every word a person reads ... in nine languages"; the drunk-grandma test (plain, readable
names on big obvious buttons); touch first (targets in reach); the commit's claim "the phone toolbar fits its four
tools".
REASON: the narrower buttons fit English only; longer words are cut with "…" and the row still overflows.
EVIDENCE: phone, Crate Game, each tool's label measured (`scrollWidth > clientWidth`): en none; zh none; es
"Deshacer", "Rehacer"; ko "되돌리기", "다시 하기"; ja "元に戻す", "やり直す"; pt "Escolher", "Desfazer"; fr
"Rétablir"; ru "Поставить", "Соединить", "Отменить", "Повторить"; de "Verbinden", "Rückgängig", "Wiederholen". In the
de and ru pictures the row reads "Verbind…", "Rückgä…" and the Redo button is cut at the screen's right edge.
EXPECTED: in every language every toolbar button is wholly on screen, at least 44 px, and its name is readable (for
example, two lines, a smaller name, or Undo and Redo as icons with their names kept for screen readers, as the design
language allows).
FIX: `Toolbar.svelte`'s phone rules only (and a word in `words/` only if a name must be shortened in all nine); a
journey or browser check at `phone` in all nine languages that no toolbar button runs past the viewport and no name is
cut.

### 3. The check for known layout issue 4 cannot fail

UNIT: `tests/journeys/settings-and-join.journey.test.ts`, test '"Where it is" stays inside the settings with the
"Opens" list open'.
VERDICT: red.
REQUIREMENT: CLAUDE.md "A check is a test that fails by itself"; plan Task 31 Step 1, issue 4: "the 'Where it is'
heading's box is inside the sheet"; spec: "a dropdown opens over the sheet without scrolling the group it belongs to out
of sight".
REASON: the test scrolls the heading into view (`scrollIntoViewIfNeeded`) before measuring it, so it is inside the
sheet whatever the sheet did; the second assertion (the heading is below the "Opens" list) only checks their order.
Nothing is opened or chosen in the list before measuring.
EVIDENCE: the test's lines `await heading.scrollIntoViewIfNeeded()` then `inside(at, panel)`.
EXPECTED: open or use the list (choose in "Opens", and the "Level" list on a door), then, without scrolling, the
heading of the group in use, and "Where it is", are in the sheet's visible area; the check fails if the sheet scrolls
them away.
FIX: that test only; code only if it then fails.
