## Red: 1

### 1. The toolbar journey's "name is cut" check cannot fail, and two names already run past their buttons

UNIT: `tests/journeys/settings-and-join.journey.test.ts`, test 'the toolbar of every language is wholly on screen, with
its names readable'.
VERDICT: red.
REQUIREMENT: CLAUDE.md "A check is a test that fails by itself"; cycle 2's red 2 FIX, "a journey or browser check at
`phone` in all nine languages that ... no name is cut".
REASON: the check compares a name's `scrollWidth` with its own `clientWidth`, but the phone rules no longer clip the
name (the `overflow: hidden` and ellipsis are gone), and as a flex item the name grows to fit its text, so the two are
always equal; a name that runs out of its button and into the next one passes.
EVIDENCE: the probe measured each visible name's text box (a `Range` over the `span`) against its button, at `phone`:
de "Verbinden" text x 170 to 222 in a button 172 to 220; ru "Поставить" 70 to 126 in 71 to 125, "Соединить" 182 to
239 in 183 to 237. Every other visible name in every language is inside its button. The spill is 1 to 2 px into a
2 px gap, so on screen (`toolbar-de-phone.png`, `toolbar-ru-phone.png`) the names still read and do not touch each
other, but nothing would catch a longer word, or a wider font, running into its neighbour.
EXPECTED: the check fails when a shown name's text is not wholly inside its own button (measure the text with a `Range`,
or the `span`'s box against the button's, allowing at most 0.5 px), in all nine languages.
FIX: that test; then `Toolbar.svelte`'s phone rules only if it fails as measured above (for example the four tools'
side padding to 0, or letting a name take two lines). Files: the journey, `src/features/shell/Toolbar.svelte`.
